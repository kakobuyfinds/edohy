How to use this KakoBuy spreadsheet guide

Find the right listing

Search by product type, filter the selection and open the CAFANS detail page. Compare photos, materials, measurements and available variants.

02 / CONFIRM

Check the buying route

Look for the original seller URL and shopping-agent options. Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the item and link. A catalog entry alone does not establish KakoBuy compatibility.

03 / REVIEW

Review before shipping

Confirm the checkout amount and relevant return terms. If using a warehouse service, inspect available QC photos and measurements before arranging international delivery.

See the whole budget

An item price is only the beginning.

Build your estimate from goods, delivery and other costs. Use amounts in the same currency. The calculator adds your own figures; it does not fetch exchange rates, agent fees or shipping quotes.

For a multi-item haul, enter the combined goods cost. Include domestic delivery, payment charges, packing, insurance and applicable taxes in the other-costs field. Keep a separate allowance for changes between your estimate and checkout.

What to check before you order

CheckWhat to compareWhy it matters
Size and fitGarment measurements, shoe insole length and selected variantA familiar size label may represent different measurements across sellers.

Sizing: compare measurements first

Measure a similar garment that already fits you. Compare chest width, body length, shoulder width and sleeves using the seller's measurement method. For trousers, compare waist, rise and inseam.

For shoes, look for the seller's size chart and insole measurement. If a crucial measurement is missing, ask for clarification before committing to the order.

QC: ask a specific question

A useful inspection request identifies exactly what you need to see: both shoes together, a size label, a close-up of stitching or a tape measurement across the chest.

Photos can help reveal visible differences, damage or an incorrect variant. They do not establish authenticity, durability or the accuracy of every material claim.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official KakoBuy website?

No. This is an independent directory and buying guide. The two main buttons open CAFANS, and the product cards open CAFANS detail pages. This page does not process orders or payments.

Are these real product listings?

Yes. The eight displayed entries were taken from the CAFANS homepage on November 19, 2026, with their listed names, image URLs, detail links and estimated USD prices. The selection is static, so confirm current availability on the linked page.

Does every listing work with KakoBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for each item. Open the product detail page, locate the original seller link and confirm that KakoBuy accepts that link and product before paying.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

No shipping-inclusive total has been verified. Treat displayed prices as source catalog estimates. Check the goods price, domestic delivery, international delivery, payment charges and applicable taxes separately.

Are the images warehouse QC photos?

No. The displayed images come from the CAFANS product catalog. If your buying service provides warehouse QC photos, review the images associated with your own order before arranging shipment.

Can I use the directory on a phone?

Yes. The product grid, category filters and calculator adapt to smaller screens. Product links remain available even when JavaScript is disabled.

Is this a downloadable spreadsheet?

This version is a searchable web directory, not an Excel file or Google Sheet. It presents a small catalog selection with direct source links and practical comparison guidance.
